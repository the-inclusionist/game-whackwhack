# Credits

## Inspiration and feature-parity reference

**[si-em/whackwhack](https://github.com/si-em/whackwhack)** — *Whack Whack — Revolution!*, by
**Simon Riisnæs Emmen**. A Dance Dance Revolution parody built over three days in October 2020.

This game reimplements its rules; it does not reuse its code. The original ships no licence, so
reuse would not have been permitted even had it been wanted — see `docs/LICENSES.md` §4. What was
taken is the shape of the game and its balance curve, both functional facts. What was left is every
line of source, the title artwork, and the styling.

Credit here is courtesy, not a licence obligation.
